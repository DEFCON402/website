Think a career in cybersecurity means becoming a hacker? Think again.

Gael Beauboeuf explores a less-talked-about but incredibly important path into cybersecurity. With more than 10 years of experience in IT and cybersecurity, Gael specializes in GRC, leading engagements across ISO 27001, NIST 800-171, SOC 2, HIPAA, and CMMC. Drawing on his journey from systems and network administration into IT audit and compliance, he'll share how others can find their way into the field without ever needing to become a penetration tester.

Whether you're a student exploring cybersecurity or considering a career change, this talk will help broaden your perspective on what's possible.

Not every cybersecurity career starts with hacking.

What happens when you bring computational biology, biohacking, and the hacker mindset together?

Jake Madison spans IT, biology, and chemistry, and has firsthand experience contributing to research on a mouse model that reported a tripling of lifespan. From computational biology to experimental research, there's still incredible science to explore — even as AI reshapes the field and research funding remains a challenge.

Jake has also seen a side of biohacking at DEF CON that looks very different from what you might expect.

What does it mean to be a hacker when the system you're exploring is biology itself?

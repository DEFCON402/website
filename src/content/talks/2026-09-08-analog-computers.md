What happens when you build a computer without a CPU?

John Calhoun spent 25 years as an engineer at Apple, then dove deep into analog computing out of pure curiosity — and built his own. He'll walk through the fundamentals of analog computing and the process of building one, with physical analog computers doing their thing along the way.

Think less PowerPoint, more science fair for computer nerds.
